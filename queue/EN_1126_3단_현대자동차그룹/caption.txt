Hyundai Motor Group carried out its CEO and president-level executive reshuffle for 2026 on the 26th, keeping the Hyundai and Kia leadership in place while changing the heads of Hyundai Mobis and other affiliates.

· Hyundai Mobis CEO: Baek Chul-seung, CEO and executive vice president of Hyundai Transys, promoted to president; he will focus on shifting the business portfolio, strengthening electrification and electronics competitiveness, and improving profitability
· Hyundai Transys CEO: Park Chan-young, executive vice president and head of Hyundai's Purchasing Division
· Hyundai Engineering CEO: Na Tae-jun, head of the Architecture Business Division, promoted to executive vice president
· Hyundai Kefico CEO: Yoo Ji-han, Hyundai's executive vice president in charge of the SDV Platform, who has worked with Forty-Two Dot on the CODA architecture and Atria AI
· Head of Hyundai domestic production and Chief Safety and Health Officer (CSO): Oh Jun-dong, CEO and executive vice president of Hyundai Kefico
· Advisors: President Lee Gyu-suk of Hyundai Mobis and President Joo Woo-jeong of Hyundai Engineering step down as CEOs and become advisors. The group's regular executive reshuffle is set for mid-December
