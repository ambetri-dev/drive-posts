Talks on approving Tesla's driver-assistance system across the EU have been pushed back to December 15.

· The Technical Committee on Motor Vehicles (TCMV) meeting planned for December 1 was postponed again with no reason given, and the committee has held two meetings since the Netherlands first approved the system in April but has not voted yet
· EU-wide approval needs at least 15 of the 27 member states in favor, and those countries must make up 65% or more of the EU population
· 12 countries, including the Netherlands and Italy, allow its use, but their combined population is only about 28.7% of the EU total
· If EU-level approval is rejected, permits already issued by individual countries could become invalid
· Tesla renamed FSD to Tesla Assisted Driving after European regulators raised concerns, and Elon Musk said he plans to develop a 6-7 seat self-driving Model Y with no steering wheel or pedals
· On the 25th in US trading, Tesla shares fell about 6%, while SpaceX shares fell 8% early in the session
