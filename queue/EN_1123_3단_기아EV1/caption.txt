According to Electrek, Kia plans to launch its most affordable entry-level EV in 2027, with the industry expecting it to be called the EV1.

· Planned at around $22,000–24,000 in the US
· Expected price in Europe: €20,000–22,000 before government subsidies
· The EV2 starts at €26,600 in Europe, and the EV3 starts at $29,890 in the US
· VP Ted Lee said Kia and Hyundai are co-developing a dedicated architecture for two entry-level EVs
· CODA, Pleos Connect and Gleo AI are planned, and Kia aims to complete its first SDV by the end of 2027
· The plan starts with highway Level 2+ support, expanding to urban Level 2++ in 2029
