New York State is banning separate subscription fees for hardware features already installed in a car at the time of purchase.

· Governor Kathy Hochul signed the bill (S.9348A/A.10411A) on the 23rd, and it is set to take effect on February 21, 2027, 90 days after enactment
· Violations carry a civil penalty of up to $250 (about KRW 336,000) per sale and per violation
· Heated seats and remote start using a physical smart key are typical examples
· Driver assistance and automated driving, Wi-Fi, satellite radio and navigation updates are exempt, so Tesla can keep charging for FSD subscriptions
· Tesla's $2,000 (about KRW 2.689 million) Acceleration Boost is a one-time purchase, so it is unclear whether the law applies
· BMW once offered heated seat subscriptions in some countries for around $18 (about KRW 24,200) a month, then withdrew them after customer backlash
