Hyundai Motor Group has unveiled the 'HMG Global AC Charger', a home-style slow charger shared across Hyundai, Kia and Genesis.

· It launches in Korea in 2027, followed by phased supply to major markets including the US, Europe and India
· The Korean version supports 7kW and 11kW output and uses the Type 1 (J1772) charging standard
· With Plug & Charge, user authentication and payment happen automatically once connected
· Separating the installation/wiring unit from the charging unit cuts installation to about 10 minutes and replacement to about 5 minutes
· It offers remote control, self-diagnosis and OTA updates, with brand app links planned for charging status, history and scheduled charging
· Hyundai and Kia led planning, technology and design, Hyundai KEFICO handles product development and mass production, and V2H and V2G bidirectional charging was considered from the design stage
