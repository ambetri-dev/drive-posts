Orders for the Volkswagen ID. Polo EV are piling up, and the base model now reportedly takes up to 21 months to deliver.

· About 30,000 ID. Polo orders secured by mid-September, with order volume nearly doubling since
· The base ID. Polo Trend takes up to 21 months to deliver, while the performance GTI wait is about 7 months
· The base model has a 37kWh battery with up to 334km of WLTP range, starting at EUR 24,995 (KRW 36 million)
· The 52kWh battery model starts at EUR 33,795 (KRW 50 million)
· With cumulative orders for the group's entry-level EVs at about 150,000, Volkswagen is reviewing production capacity changes, including shifting combustion-engine production resources to EVs
