Korean-made cars' share of Korea's passenger EV market fell to 48.7% in January–September this year, dropping below 50%.

· 112,417 new Chinese-made passenger EVs registered in Jan–Sep, more than double the same period last year
· Chinese-made EVs took 43.5% of all 258,284 passenger EVs
· Chinese share reached 33.9% last year, up 32.8 percentage points from 1.1% in 2021, the third-largest rise among the world's top 10 car-producing countries after Mexico and Brazil
· Tesla sold 98,313 units in Jan–Oct, up 105.0% year on year, and the Model Y ranked No. 1 in import sales with 78,631 registrations
· BYD sold over 20,000 units in Jan–Oct, while Korea's auto trade deficit with China hit USD 4,230.56 million, up 92.7% year on year
· Industry and the National Assembly are considering a tax credit for Korean-built EVs, and KAMA chairman Jung Dae-jin stressed the need for a production tax credit plus R&D and facility investment support
