Toyota has shown the BKE concept, a folding electric two-wheeler that fits in a trunk, under its revived Scion brand.

· It folds in about 30 seconds and is small enough to fit in the trunk of the GR86 compact sports car
· It has no pedals; the rider sits on the seat and moves on electric motor power
· It can be charged from the 110V AC outlet in some Toyota models
· A small team of chassis and electrical engineers developed it over several years, and the rear fender was enlarged after a comment from Chairman Akio Toyoda
· Specs such as range, top speed, battery capacity and motor output have not been released, and production, timing and price are still undecided
· At a recent event, Toyota also showed the KEI-01 compact utility truck and a hybrid off-road vehicle
