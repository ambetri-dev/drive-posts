Uber began piloting 'Uber Kids', a ride-hailing service that children aged 10 to 12 can ride alone, on November 19.

· The pilot runs in parts of Austin, Nashville and Madison in the US
· Only a registered guardian can request a ride, and only guardians can set or change the destination
· A guardian or registered adult must be present when the child gets in and out
· Drivers are background-checked and highly rated
· A live streaming feature shows the inside of the car; recorded audio and video are stored encrypted, reviewed only if a safety issue is reported, and deleted after 14 days if nothing happens
· A pickup PIN code is also part of the safety setup, and Uber plans to check how much the PIN code and live video reassure parents
