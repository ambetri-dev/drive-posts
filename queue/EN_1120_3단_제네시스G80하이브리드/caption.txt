Genesis has given the G80 Hybrid its world premiere at the LA Auto Show.

· Unveiled on the 19th (local time) at the Los Angeles Convention Center, USA
· Combined efficiency of 14.1 km/L, about 34% better than the 2.5 gasoline turbo (18-inch 2WD, in-house test)
· Up to about 1,029 km on a single tank
· Wheelbase stretched by 130 mm for more cabin space
· Response at 100 km/h about 18% better than the 2.5 gasoline turbo
· Actively uses EV mode from a standstill up to about 30 km/h
