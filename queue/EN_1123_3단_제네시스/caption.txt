Genesis is moving GV70 production from its Montgomery, Alabama plant in the US to Hyundai's Ulsan Plant in Korea.

· Major international media reported the move on the 23rd (local time).
· Local US production will stop, and Ulsan-built cars will be exported to the US instead.
· US sales will continue, supplied with vehicles built in Korea.
· Genesis's North America COO explained that rising Hyundai sales in the US left the Alabama plant short on production capacity.
· The electrified GV70 had been built at the Alabama plant since February 2023, the first time Genesis produced a complete vehicle outside Korea.
· Electrified GV70 production in Alabama was paused last August, and an industry source said there is still room for local US production to restart.
